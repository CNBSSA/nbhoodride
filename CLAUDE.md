## What this product is

**PGRide** — ride-sharing for Prince George's County, MD (USA): riders, driver-partners, Stripe/cash fares, wallet ledger, driver equity program, super-admin ops. Stack: Node.js/TypeScript, React, PostgreSQL, Railway (`nbhoodride-production.up.railway.app`).

## Workflow

Develop on `develop`; promote to `main` only with Festus's explicit approval after testing. Planning → change-impact audit → implement → test → post-implementation audit.

**Promotion timing (standing instruction, Festus 2026-09-01):** promotions to `main` are batched to the daily quiet window (~03:30 ET / 07:30 UTC) so deploy restarts never blip riders during riding hours. Merge reviewed work to `develop` any time; it ships to `main` in the next window. Exception: urgent rider-blocking fixes promote immediately.


**Rider Promise Review (Festus 2026-09-07):** "reliable" means a rider who books a ride gets picked up, on time, at the quoted price — every time. The numbers are defined in `shared/riderPromise.ts` (rides delivered, strandings, fare accuracy, late pickups, plus what is unclaimed in the next 24h) and computed in `server/riderPromiseReview.ts`. The server posts it to the ops Telegram chat once a day at 4:00 AM Eastern; it is separate from, and named differently to, the GitHub "Daily Reliability Report" (code health). Admin: `GET/POST /api/admin/analytics/rider-promise-review`.

**Proactive reliability (Festus 2026-09-09):** "reliable" also means every feature, menu and button works, and the operator hears about trouble before the rider does. `server/rideRiskWatch.ts` (rules in `shared/rideRisk.ts`) pages the ops Telegram when a scheduled ride is still unclaimed at T-2h and T-15m, and once at T-10 when the driver has no fresh position or is more than 4 miles from the pickup (stamps o120/o15/o10 in `rides.reminder_stamps`; admin `POST /api/admin/analytics/ride-risk-sweep`). Every rider alert, including app crashes from the error boundary (`client_crash`), is also written to `reliability_events`, and the Rider Promise Review reports "App health" and "Paged ahead" lines from it. Production is watched two ways: `server/dependencyWatch.ts` checks the database and Stripe every 10 minutes from inside the server and pages on down/recovered (`GET /health/deps`, admin `POST /api/admin/analytics/dependency-check`); `.github/workflows/production-watch.yml` probes from outside every 10 minutes (`npm run watch:production`) and pages Telegram on a change when the repo secrets `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` / `TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID` are set. Every pull request also runs the every-button audit (`npm run test:buttons`, `scripts/e2e/button-audit.mjs`): as visitor, rider, driver and admin it opens every screen and presses every safe button, failing on a crash, blank screen, red toast or 5xx. Every `data-testid="button-…"` in `client/src` must be pressed by it or listed in `scripts/e2e/button-audit-baseline.json` with a reason, so no new button ships untested.

**Commercial riders (Festus 2026-09-10, plan: "Commercial Riders" artifact):** organizations (a dialysis center, an office, a restaurant) book rides and deliveries for other people and are billed; the business places every order, the person served never holds an account. Built as a modular monolith in vertical slices behind `COMMERCIAL_ENABLED` (off in production until a slice is proven). One spine: every job is a row in `rides` (`payment_method = 'invoice'`, `ride_type = 'commercial'`, passenger by name/phone) plus a `commercial_jobs` row; one door: `server/commercial/` and routes under `/api/admin/organizations/*` and `/api/org/*`, every member route through `requireMember` scoping to the organization; pure rules and statement math in `shared/commercial.ts`. Paging for a commercial job names the account and job number, never the passenger. Slice 1 (organizations, book for a client, monthly statement) shipped first; slices 2–6 (portal, standing orders, badges, weekly billing, deliveries) follow in order. Slice 2: the requester portal at `/org` (`client/src/pages/portal/PortalPage.tsx`) is desktop first and installable from the browser (manifest orientation any, a wide screenshot, `usePwaInstallPrompt`): the next 48 hours as a board with a map, all jobs with cancel, the statement, and people (owners); `N` opens the booking form, `Ctrl+Enter` books. Member routes added: `GET /api/org/:orgId`, `POST/DELETE /api/org/:orgId/members` (owners), `POST /api/org/:orgId/jobs/:jobId/cancel` (bookers; rider ladder until slice 3; a job on the road cannot be cancelled from the desk). The harness seeds a standing organization (`e2e-org`, rider as owner) so the every-button audit (requester role) and the layout audit's desk-and-phone pass can open the portal.
